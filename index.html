Larry

Larry is a small tool that prepares slideshow drafts for our own TikTok accounts: dailywalletfix and the channels for our iPhone apps ShiftPayday and Glovebox. It uploads finished slides to the TikTok inbox as drafts. A person then adds a sound and decides whether to post.

Larry is not offered to other users. Questions: open an issue at github.com/HamzaBenFredj/larry/issues.
