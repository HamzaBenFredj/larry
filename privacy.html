Privacy Policy

When a TikTok account is connected, Larry receives that account's basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and an access token. These are stored only on the operator's own computer and are used for one purpose: uploading slideshow drafts to that account.

Larry does not collect data about other TikTok users, does not sell or share data, and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. The slide images published on this site are the drafts themselves.

To remove Larry's access, revoke it in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions. Questions: github.com/HamzaBenFredj/larry/issues.
